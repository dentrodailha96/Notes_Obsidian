**"No matter how isolated you are and how lonely you feel, if you do your work truly and conscientiously, unknown friends will come and seek you"- Carl Jung**

"**I’m not gonna lie to you, a lot of people in Bali were not the type of people I’d want as friends.**

**I was there travelling alone, and I really wanted to meet nice people with whom I could connect with.**

**So what would be the easie choice for me to take?**

**To change myself into what I thought most people there would like.**

To go to the parties they went to

To pretend to like stuff I don’t but most people there do

That way I would have probably made more friends in there.

But they wouldn’t be the people I wanted around anyway.

So I stayed true to who I was and this way I found people that were actually aligned with me."
