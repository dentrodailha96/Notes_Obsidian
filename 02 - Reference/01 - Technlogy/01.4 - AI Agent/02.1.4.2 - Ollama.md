### Ollama models and their difference

https://www.morphllm.com/best-ollama-models#model-list

-  Each model is specialist in something. Which means, if I need something to complete a sentence it will focus in NLP, if we need something to analyse the image it will focus in Deep Learning and it goes on. 
- There are mainly 5 models: Cloud, Embedding, Vision, Tools and Thinking (market).

Source: https://aiwithkatarina.com/ollama-model-types/

|**If you want to…**|**Choose this type:**|
|---|---|
|Chat, write emails, or summarize text on a standard PC|**Standard Text Models**|
|Analyze photos, charts, or diagrams|**Vision**|
|Connect your AI to the internet or local apps|**Tools**|
|Solve complex coding bugs or logic puzzles|**Thinking**|
|Search through thousands of personal documents|**Embedding**|
