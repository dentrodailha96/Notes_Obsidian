
Flow attention - state in which are much less dependent on will and effort, related to interest. [...] Focused attention is different from "sustained attention", which we need to stay focused on one task for a longer period and is necessary to learn, understand or get something done.

Focus attention - something that requires willpower to sustain.[...]when it comes to focused attention, we focus on one thing only, something we can sustain for only a few seconds. Doyle and Zakrajsek 2013.

Proofreading - find the right words. When we proofread a manuscript, we take on the role of a critic who takes a step back to see the text with the eyes of a dispassionate reader.
Critic - it is enough to spot most of what we missed before: the wholes in the argument, the part we did not explain as we did not need to explain them to ourselves.

While proofreading requires more focused attention, finding the right words during writing requires much more floating attention.
